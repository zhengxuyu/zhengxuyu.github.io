I'm excited to share [OneManCompany (OMC)](https://github.com/1mancompany/OneManCompany), an open-source agentic operating system designed for individuals who want to build and run AI-powered companies solo.

The core idea is simple: you are the CEO, and AI agents are your employees. OMC orchestrates hierarchical teams of AI agents that collaborate autonomously on complex projects — from engineering and design to operations and strategy. Instead of hiring a full team, you delegate to agents that communicate, review each other's work, and deliver iteratively through structured approval chains.

OMC supports multiple execution backends including Claude Code, OpenRouter, and OpenClaw. Its modular architecture separates vessels (runtime environments) from talents (agent capabilities), making it straightforward to customize agent roles and workflows for different types of projects.

Key features include multi-agent meetings for collaborative decision-making, a talent marketplace for discovering and hiring specialized agents, performance reviews to maintain output quality, and quality gates that ensure iterative delivery meets your standards before shipping.

If you've ever felt bottlenecked trying to do everything yourself, or wished you could spin up a capable team on demand without the overhead of actual hiring, OMC is built for exactly that scenario. Check out the [GitHub repo](https://github.com/1mancompany/OneManCompany) and give it a try.
